Description complète

Join us in pioneering breakthroughs in healthcare. For everyone. Everywhere. Sustainably.

Our inspiring and caring environment forms a global community that celebrates diversity and individuality. We encourage you to step beyond your comfort zone, offering resources and flexibility to foster your professional and personal growth, all while valuing your unique contributions.

Apply now for the position of Product Verification Engineer, where you will apply your clinical experience in radiotherapy to system-level verification, helping ensure that our medical device systems support safe and effective clinical workflows. Your role

  • You apply your radiotherapy experience to develop realistic test scenarios that reflect clinical workflows and intended use.
  • You contribute to planning system-level verification for product releases and projects, considering requirements, design changes, risks and regression impact.
  • You design and review test cases with a focus on clinically relevant workflows and perform hands-on formal and informal verification testing on complex medical device systems.
  • You analyze test results, document complete and traceable objective evidence, report defects and support troubleshooting and problem analysis together with engineering teams.
  • You contribute to requirement, design and risk reviews and collaborate with local and international stakeholders from Engineering, Product Management, Risk Management and other relevant areas.
  • You contribute to the continuous improvement of verification methods, tools and processes and support knowledge sharing across the organization.
  • You are willing to travel up to 10% as required.

Your expertise

  • A degree or equivalent professional qualification in a relevant clinical discipline, medical technology or a comparable field.
  • Several years of hands-on clinical experience in radiotherapy, with a strong understanding of clinical workflows and the practical use of radiotherapy systems.
  • Ability to translate clinical workflows and practical experience into structured test scenarios and clearly documented results.
  • An analytical, structured and proactive approach, with the willingness to build technical expertise and work independently on complex systems.
  • Strong commitment to product safety and quality.
  • Fluent spoken and written English
  • German is an advantage.
  • Experience creating radiotherapy treatment plans is an advantage.
  • Experience in medical device verification or structured system testing is an advantage.
