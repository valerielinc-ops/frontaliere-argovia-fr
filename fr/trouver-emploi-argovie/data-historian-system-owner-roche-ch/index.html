The Position

About the Role

We are looking for a Data Historian System Owner to lead the operational reliability, maintenance, and long-term evolution of our site Data Historian landscape across Basel and Kaiseraugst. In this role, you will oversee key operational and monitoring systems, including Aveva PI Platforms and Rotronic Monitoring Systems (RMS) ensuring high availability, system performance, and regulatory compliance while supporting our site's digital maturity in line with the PT Strategy. You will bridge IT and OT domains and support end-to-end Make Assess Release (MAR) processes in a dynamic pharmaceutical manufacturing environment.

The Opportunity

  • System Operations & Management: Oversee the daily operations of site Data Historian systems to guarantee high availability, reliability, and robust performance. Systems in scope include PI Monitoring Systems and Platforms for Basel and Kaiseraugst, as well as Rotronic Monitoring Systems (RMS) across both sites.
  • Maintenance & Troubleshooting: Plan and execute routine maintenance, updates, and system upgrades. Promptly diagnose and resolve complex technical issues related to the Data Historian infrastructure.
  • Architecture & Strategy Alignment: Oversee the IT/OT technological landscape and its integration across the site, supporting the digital maturity goals of the PT Strategy and end-to-end Make Assess Release (MAR) processes.
  • Optimization & Innovation: Design and implement performance optimization strategies to enhance data collection, retrieval, and analytics. Stay abreast of emerging Data Historian technology trends and drive continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Stakeholder Collaboration: Partner closely with Process Owners, process engineers, IT teams, Business Integration Managers, and Technical Product Leads to align technical capabilities with operational requirements and business opportunities.
  • Compliance & Documentation: Maintain strict adherence to GxP standards, Safety, Health and Environment (SHE) regulations, and internal company policies. Ensure comprehensive documentation of system configurations and operating procedures.

Who you are

  • Education: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Biology, Mathematics, Electrical Engineering, Information Systems, or a related field (Master’s or PhD is a plus).
  • Professional Experience: 5+ years of professional experience in global and complex business environments, with proven IT/OT domain expertise in the pharmaceutical or biotech industry, particularly within manufacturing and Technical Operations.
  • Technical Expertise: Proficiency in Data Historian platforms, specifically Aveva PI and Rotronic RMS . Solid understanding of SQL databases, network infrastructure, and industrial communication protocols. Practical experience working with data visualization and analytics tools. Sound knowledge of pharmaceutical technologies and processes, including digital plant, shop floor automation, quality lab systems, and overall system architecture integration.
  • Methodology & Mindset: An entrepreneurial problem solver who applies lean and agile principles, focusing on pragmatic solutions and a "Reuse before Buy before Make" philosophy.
  • Soft Skills & Leadership: Strong negotiation, analytical, and interpersonal skills with the ability to communicate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders across global and local levels. Demonstrated ability to manage ambiguity, foster trust, and champion a speak-up culture.
  • Language Proficiency: Proficient in both German and English .
