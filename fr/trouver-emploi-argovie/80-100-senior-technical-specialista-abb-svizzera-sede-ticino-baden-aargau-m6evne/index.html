Description complète

At ABB, we help industries run leaner and cleaner—and every person here makes that happen. You’ll be empowered to lead, supported to grow, and proud of the impact we create together. Join us and help run what runs the world.

This position reports to:

Senior Engineering Manager

__

In this role, you will have the opportunity to lead technical expertise assignments of high complexity within the area of Drive Applications. Each day, you will contribute to advancing our electric drive solutions and services for heavy-duty minerals processing equipment—such as mills, high-pressure grinding rolls, conveyors, fans, and pumps—while helping shape our technical strategy.

The work model for the role is: #LI-Hybrid

This role is contributing to the Process Industries division in Switzerland (located in Baden-City). Main stakeholders are R&D, Product Management, the Quality department, suppliers, and engineering teams.

You will be mainly accountable for:

  • Driving solution advancement: Collaborating closely with R&D and Product Management to co-develop next-generation drive solutions, define technical standards, and translate field insights into product improvements.
  • Leading L3/L4 escalation and RCA: Acting as the senior technical authority by leading complex troubleshooting, systematic root cause analysis (RCA), and critical issue resolution.
  • Managing supplier and quality interfaces: Partnering with the Quality department and key suppliers to investigate component failures, validate corrective actions, and ensure long-term system reliability.
  • Developing team competency: Elevating technical expertise across the engineering team by mentoring colleagues, identifying training needs, and sharing specialized knowledge.

Our team dynamics
You will join a highly motivated, collaborative, and innovative team, where you will be able to thrive and make a lasting technical impact.


• Qualifications for the role:

  • Educational background: You hold a B.Sc. or M.Sc. in Electrical or Automation Engineering with a strong emphasis on Power Electronics and Drive Technology.
  • Deep domain expertise: You have 10+ years of experience working with electric drive systems and power electronics—ideally within heavy industries or minerals processing.
  • Advanced troubleshooting skills: You possess a proven track record in L3/L4 technical support, complex system diagnostics, and structured root cause analysis (RCA) across hardware, software, and application interfaces.
  • Cross-functional and supplier collaboration: You are highly adept at working across R&D, Product Management, Quality, and external component suppliers to resolve technical bottlenecks and drive continuous improvement.
  • Mentorship and innovation mindset: You are passionate about coaching colleagues, sharing knowledge, and translating complex technical challenges into scalable engineering solutions.
  • Communication and flexibility: You are at ease communicating in English at an advanced technical level (written and spoken) and are willing to travel occasionally (approx. 10–20%) to customer sites or suppliers for critical escalations.
