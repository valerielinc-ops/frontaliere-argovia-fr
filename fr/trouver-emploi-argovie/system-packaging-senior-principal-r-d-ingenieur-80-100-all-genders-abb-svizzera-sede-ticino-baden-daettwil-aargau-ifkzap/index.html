Your role and responsibilities

We are looking for an enthusiastic and experienced System Packaging / Industrial R&D Engineer to join our Direct Current Data Center Incubator, playing a critical role in driving the transformational shift toward next-generation Direct current data center architectures. In this role you will work alongside the ABB DC Architecture offering of the new ABB Infinitus Direct Current (DC) Portfolio for Datacenters team.

In this role, you will lead the Power Electronics Systems Packaging integration solutions, including close interaction with system design, control HW, testing, validation, productization and certification for the new ABB Infinitus Direct Current Architecture products offering for Datacenters.

The work model for the role is: onsite/hybrid

This role is contributing to the Direct Current Data Center Incubator in Europe.

You will be mainly accountable for:

  • Leading the Power Electronic Systems Packaging solutions development, implementation, validation and certification. The tasks cover the phases from the conceptual design of packaging solutions for indoor and outdoor environments, to their implementation and final validation and certification.
  • Closely collaborate with the different stakeholders in the organization of the ABB Infinitus Direct Current (DC) Portfolio: program manager, product architect, research scientist, industrialization and productization teams, etc.
  • Contribute to the maintenance and further development of the key Power Electronics System Integration solutions, Expertise and Know-How of the DC Data Center Incubator R&D team, including IP and Standards activities.
  • You will be part of advanced R&D activities in the field of power converter systems for the ABB DC Data Center Products, in close cooperation with a highly qualified senior team of R&D engineers and scientists in a global and multicultural setup.




 • Qualifications for the role

  • University Master degree or PhD in the field of industrial / mechanical engineering or equivalent experience in systems packaging solutions of high power multi-megawatt and medium voltage Power Electronics converter systems.
  • Solid background experience (> 5 years) in High Power Electronics System packaging solutions with focus on mechanical structure and cooling system design, implementation and validation according to indoor and outdoor environmental requirement conditions (temperature, humidity, shock and vibration, seismic, etc.). This includes expertise and practical experience with mechanical development tools and processes.
  • Experience in design, modelling, and simulation of electro-thermo–mechanical systems using state of the art simulation and engineering design tools.
  • High interest and focus on practical implementation and validation in MV environment and new products and functionalities’ introduction to the market. Expertise in validation, productization and certification of power electronic converters’ packaging solutions is desired.
  • Interest in applied research and development, self-organized, motivated person, that likes to work in a highly qualified research and development team composed of experts in different domains and global locations.
  • Fluency in written and spoken English. German is a plus.
  • Availability to travel sporadically.




More about us

ABB's Distribution Solutions Division facilitates the efficient and reliable distribution, protection and control of power by improving electric power quality while strengthening the resilience of the grid. The Division offers segment-specific products and solutions that largely serve utilities, industry and infrastructure segments, often providing the requisite medium-voltage link between high-voltage transmission systems and low-voltage users. With ABB Ability™ enabled connected solutions at its core, the offering includes medium-voltage air- and gas-insulated switchgear, indoor and outdoor circuit breakers, reclosers, fuses, contactors, relays, instrument transformers, sensors, motor control centers, as well as low-voltage switchgear for the ANSI standard markets.
