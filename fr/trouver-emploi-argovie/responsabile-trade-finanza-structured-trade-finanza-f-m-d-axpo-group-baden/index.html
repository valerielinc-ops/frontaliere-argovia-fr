Description complète

Location: Baden

Shape the future of trade finance and structured trade finance at Axpo. Lead a talented team, drive innovative financing strategies and support the Group’s global commercial ambitions.

What you will do

  • Lead the Trade Finance and Structured Trade Finance team (four persons in total), reporting to the Head of Group Treasury, and develop and execute a strategy aligned with Axpo's internal and external goals.
  • Act as strategic finance expert for all commercial teams and group companies across power, gas and other products, covering short- to long-term working capital solutions ranging from standard to highly structured products (guarantees, LCs, inventories, receivables/payables, factoring, supply chain finance programs, pre/post-payments and more).
  • Play a leading role in setting up and enhancing banking facilities in Switzerland and abroad, acting as the primary link between internal teams and external partners such as banks, lawyers and advisors.
  • Develop new commercial strategies and expand the customer base together with trading teams, balancing reward and risk while leveraging the Group's products, expertise and global reach.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with 30+ external partners (banks, insurers) and internal stakeholders across Europe, the US and Asia.
  • Drive change, enhance efficiency and collaboration, advance the digitalisation and automation of finance processes and identify practical opportunities to apply AI.

What you bring & who you are

  • University degree or equivalent education in Finance or Law, with solid knowledge of structured commodity finance and risk.
  • Strong knowledge of the commodities market, especially the European gas and power market.
  • Around 10 years of experience in structured commodity finance, gained within a reputable commodity bank or international commodity trading house, with a strong track record in financial structuring and business partnering.
  • Proven experience of at least 5 years leading a team.
  • Hands-on experience with a Treasury Management System (TMS).
  • Strong communicator with an entrepreneurial, analytical mindset and high integrity, team-oriented and able to manage, sell and execute new concepts.
  • You use AI tools, including AxpoGPT – the Axpo Group's internal AI platform with access to multiple AI models – thoughtfully and responsibly to boost your efficiency.
  • Fluent in English – German and other European languages are a plus.

Even if you don't tick every box, we'd still love to hear from you – diverse experiences and perspectives make our team stronger. Eligibility to work in Switzerland is required. Please note that we do not accept applications submitted through recruitment agencies.

About the team

You'll join a team at the heart of Axpo's commercial operations, working closely with trading, risk, legal and finance colleagues across the globe. We value diverse perspectives, open collaboration and the drive to keep improving together.
