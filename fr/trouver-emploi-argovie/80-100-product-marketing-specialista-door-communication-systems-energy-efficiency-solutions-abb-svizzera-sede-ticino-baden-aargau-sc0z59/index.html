Description complète

At ABB, we help industries run leaner and cleaner—and every person here makes that happen. You’ll be empowered to lead, supported to grow, and proud of the impact we create together. Join us and help run what runs the world.

This position reports to:

EL Product Marketing Manager

__

In this role, you will implement product marketing strategies in your assigned areas, aligned with the global product strategy and business objectives. You will carry out marketing activities and support the sales organization in promoting sales and services for the relevant products. You will also use your expertise to improve sales efficiency by providing product knowledge, information, and training.

This role is contributing to the Smart Building Division in Switzerland. Main stakeholders are Product Marketing, Sales Department, Customers

You will join a high performing team, where you will be able to thrive.

The work model for the role is: #LI-Hybrid

You will be mainly accountable for:

  • Implementing the product marketing strategy in the assigned area/segment, including defining and communicating product value propositions and product positioning in accordance with the global product marketing strategy.
  • Achieving product marketing targets and budgets by overseeing progress regularly and defining and implementing improvement plans as needed.
  • Focusing on achievement of orders and driving continuous improvement in the Net Promoter Score (NPS).
  • Executing annual marketing plan in collaboration with relevant division and/or product groups.
  • Developing business plans focused on driving business development for a defined market.

• Qualifications for the role:

  • You are highly skilled in go -to- market strategies and have a deep understanding of the Swiss building market.
  • You can demonstrate advanced skills and have at least three years of product marketing experience, with a proven track record.
  • You are qualified in SAP, Salesforce, and relevant product marketing and finance processes
  • You are passionate about go-to-market strategies and driving your portfolio to the next level
  • You have proven experience in product marketing and product portfolio strategy for electrification products, across marketing and sales.
  • You are fluent in German and English. French is an advantage.
  • You hold a valid Swiss visa or work permit
